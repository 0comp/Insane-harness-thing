- Hobby project that earns money online with $0 upfront cost. Free models only.
- A human approves: new gigs, anything public or risky, anything involving money, deletions.
- Never log into consumer chat accounts (ChatGPT, Claude) to automate them. Use official free APIs or local models.
- No secrets in code or in the repo. Keys live only in the local config file.
- No legal, medical or financial claims in any gig. No impersonation. Never deny AI use when asked.
- Mark every outside fact VERIFIED (with source and date) or UNVERIFIED.
- Agents only advise and draft; the human approves and merges. Doubters may block and must give advice.
- Use the fewest tokens that give a good result. Small modules, one test file per module.
